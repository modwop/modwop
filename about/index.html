About

modwop.com showcases live electronic dance music artists on Eurorack & Modular systems (without computers or DJs).

With a mission to teach, perform and grow the art of modular synthesis, including workshops on topics such as: music synthesis, building a modular system, making music with modular systems, or soldering Eurorack kits.

Eurorack

Eurorack is a global modular synthesizer standard created by Dieter Doepfer.

The terms "Modular" and "Eurorack" are often used interchangeably, but there are many other modular standards and form factors, with many Eurorack module designers & manufacturers all over the world; From DIY hobbyists, to single person shops, to dedicated Eurorack module companies.

The Art

The musical art that is derived from a modular system starts with an idea for a module that aims to make, manipulate or modulate sound in a unique manner.

That idea is first carefully planned and designed onto a circuit board.

The front panel of the module is the interface to the unique circuits, sometimes with simple labels, or a work of art by itself.

Each artist's modular system is a one-of-a-kind musical instrument, as each module has to be selected, placed and planned on how the instrument will be patched together and played.

The musical performance is the final artistic sum of it all.
